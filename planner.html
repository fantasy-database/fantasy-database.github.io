    Expected points are not in here yet. The colours come from the fixture model — they tell you whose run of games looks kind, not how many points he will score. Judging a transfer properly needs a points projection per player per gameweek, which is the next piece of work. Until then this is a squad you can build, save and read fixtures off.

    Your squad is saved in this browser only. It never leaves your computer, and it is not connected to your real FPL team — loading that in directly is something the FPL API does not allow a site like this one to do. Prices are today's prices, so the totals here describe a squad bought today rather than the money in your own account.
