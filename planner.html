      How the expected points work. A player's xP for a gameweek starts from how likely he is to play and for how long, then takes his usual share of his side's chances and sets it against how many goals his side should score and concede in that fixture — the same fixture model as the ticker. Clean sheets, saves, defensive contributions, cards and bonus are added the same way. Tuned on 2024/25 and then tested week by week through 2025/26, it had lower error than points per game or recent form one, four and eight weeks ahead, and the 20 players it rated highest each week outscored theirs. It is an average, not a prediction: a 6 xP player still blanks often.
