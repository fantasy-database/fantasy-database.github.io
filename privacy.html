Privacy

Fantasy DataBase has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies, and sells nothing about you.

Last updated 24 October 2026

  • No sign-up. You never give this site your name, email or FPL password.
  • No cookies. The site sets none, so there is no cookie banner to click through.
  • Your squad stays on your device. Planner squads, drafts and settings are saved in your own browser, not on any server of ours.
  • Visits are counted, not tracked. We see how many people use each page, not who they are.

Who runs this site

Fantasy DataBase (fantasydatabase.app) is an independent fan site run by one person in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK data protection law, that person is the controller of the small amount of information described below.

Counting visits

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused visit counter, to see which pages are used. It keeps daily totals per page, broken down by the site you came from, browser, operating system, screen width, language and country. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not store your IP address or any tracking ID. To avoid counting the same visit twice it briefly holds your IP address and browser details in memory, for up to 8 hours, and then discards them. Nothing is combined with other data or used for advertising. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

If you block the counter with an ad or tracker blocker, the site still works exactly the same.

What your browser stores

Some features remember your choices using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it:

  • the two teams you last picked in the Fixtures page's pair builder;
  • your Planner squad, plans and saved drafts;
  • the FPL team ID you last loaded, so you don't have to type it again.

These are only saved because you used the feature. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Loading your FPL team

When you type a team ID into the Planner, your browser asks the official Fantasy Premier League API for that team's public picks, season history and transfers, plus public player stats: the same information anyone can see on the FPL website. FPL does not let other sites ask for it directly, so the request passes through a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers. The relay only forwards the request; beyond a one-minute cache of the public FPL response, it stores nothing and we keep no record of team IDs. Cloudflare, like any network provider, handles your IP address to deliver the request and may keep standard technical logs under its own policy. We never ask for, see or store your FPL login.

Other services involved

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, logs visitor IP addresses for security and keeps them under its own policy. We do not have access to those logs.
  • Fonts. The site's fonts are served from this site itself, so loading a page does not contact Google or any other font provider.
  • Buy Me a Coffee. The ☕ Support button takes you to Buy Me a Coffee, a separate site with its own privacy policy. If you choose to support the site, we see only what you choose to share there, such as a name and message; payment details are handled by them and never reach us.

Why this is allowed, and how long it is kept

Counting visits and running the team-loading relay rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the site working and understanding which parts are useful, in a way that has minimal impact on you. GoatCounter's aggregated statistics are kept while the site runs. Nothing else is kept by us at all.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. In practice we hold nothing that identifies you, but you are welcome to ask. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

Questions about this page can be sent through the site's GitHub page or as a message on our Buy Me a Coffee page.

Ratings and projections are estimates, not advice.
